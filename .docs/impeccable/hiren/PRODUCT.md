# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

**Primary: people deciding whether Hiren is the real thing.** Hiring managers, founders, fellow engineers, and prospective collaborators who land on `hiren.pravritti.org` after a referral, a LinkedIn click, or a search. They arrive skeptical and impatient — they've seen a hundred lookalike dev portfolios. Their job-to-be-done is fast: *"In 20 seconds, is this person credible, and is this someone I'd want to work with?"* They're often on mobile, often mid-scroll between other tabs.

Hiren is a QA Automation Lead with 10+ years in software quality engineering, based in Sutrapada. First dedicated QA hire at Vividly (pioneered AI tooling in QA), grew QA from scratch at TestGorilla (0%→40% Playwright coverage), led a team of 4 at Blackhawk (84% coverage). Deep, hands-on: Playwright, TypeScript, Python, CI/CD, Docker, cloud. He is an individual under the Pravritti brand — the site carries his name, not a firm's.

## Product Purpose

`hiren.pravritti.org` is Hiren's home base on the web: the single polished place he points people to. Its job is **credibility and presence**, not lead capture — there's no hard funnel. Success is a visitor coming away with two clear impressions: *this person genuinely knows quality engineering at depth*, and *I'd actually want to talk to them*. The site should make a strong, memorable impression in the first fold and reward a full scroll, while staying fast and accessible on any device.

This is the first site built in the Pravritti multi-subdomain platform (the apex AI-lab homepage, `tithi` app landing, and `saptapar` game come later, each in its own spec).

## Brand Personality

**Warm · sharp · genuine.** First-person, no fluff — Hiren talking the way a senior engineer actually talks, with personality and a bit of humor. Confident because the track record earns it, never because the copy oversells. The interface itself has a playful, interactive, hands-on quality (cursor-reactive, alive) that mirrors how he works: an engineer who likes to *poke at things and break them on purpose*. Approachable over corporate, human over polished. Light and bright, not moody.

## Anti-references

What this must NOT be:

- **A formatted resume.** No CV-as-webpage — no skills bars, no dry reverse-chronological bullet timeline as the centerpiece. It should feel like an experience, not a document. (The resume exists; this is not it.)
- **A generic SaaS template.** No Inter + gradient-blob + identical icon-card-grid landing page. If it could be mistaken for AI-generated boilerplate, it has failed.
- **Corporate / agency stiff.** No stock-photo professionalism, no "we deliver solutions" voice, no soulless polish. Warmth is the whole point.
- **A dark developer-portfolio cliché.** No dark-mode neon terminal, matrix rain, or monospace-as-costume. Explicitly **light, not dark**.
- **The previously rejected build:** designer-minimal — lots of empty whitespace, plain bordered text chips, grey placeholder photos, timid subtle reveals. Read as unfinished and boring. Do not equate "minimal" with "good" here.

## Design Principles

1. **Experience, not document.** Every section should justify being a *web page* rather than a PDF — through motion, interaction, or composition that a resume can't do. If a section is just bullets, redesign it.
2. **Proof carries the credibility.** Real numbers and real history (0→40%, 84% coverage, first QA hire, AI tooling in QA) do the persuading. Show the track record; don't make adjective-claims about it. Don't manufacture metrics that aren't his.
3. **Sound like Hiren.** Warm, direct, first-person, a little funny. The voice is the differentiator a template can't copy. Cut any sentence a marketing page would write.
4. **Motion with intent, never as a tax.** Interactivity should delight and feel alive, but content is readable and the site is usable the instant it loads — and just as usable with motion off. Playfulness never blocks comprehension or access.
5. **Distinctive on purpose.** The bar is "how was this made?", not "which AI made this?". Commit to a specific, memorable point of view rather than the safe modal portfolio.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Solid baseline, non-negotiable given the motion-forward direction:

- **WCAG 2.1 AA** contrast for all text (body ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1), verified — not assumed.
- **`prefers-reduced-motion`** honored everywhere: heavy/interactive motion degrades to calm crossfades or instant states, and the page is fully usable with motion suppressed. Content is never gated behind a reveal that could fail to fire.
- **Keyboard-navigable**: every interactive element reachable and operable, visible focus states.
- **Color is never the only signal** — pair it with text, icon, or shape (matters for the metric/data visuals).
- Responsive and legible from small mobile up; touch targets adequate.
